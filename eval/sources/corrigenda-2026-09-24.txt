Erratum to "2MTF I. The Tully-Fisher Relation in the 2MASS J, H and K Bands"
(Masters et al. 2008). A sign error was found in the application of internal
extinction and k-corrections to the magnitudes: the corrections were positive,
dimming the magnitudes, rather than negative. The average magnitude error over
all 888 galaxies in the sample was 0.34 mag in J, 0.18 mag in H and 0.15 mag
in K.

Erratum, MNRAS 511, 1224, "Revisiting the Cygnus OB associations". After
publication a small error was identified in the code processing the Gaia
proper motions. The correction produced a number of small modifications to
the numerical results, but did not change the main or qualitative results.
